Laser meter check

This page reads what your Bluetooth laser meter sends, so we can make it work with YOSIDI CAD. It takes about two minutes and three measurements.

  1. Switch the meter on, with its Bluetooth enabled, next to this computer.
  2. Close YOSIDI CAD and any other app that uses the meter.
  3. If the meter is paired with your iPad or phone, turn Bluetooth off there for now. The meter talks to one device at a time.

When you press the button, your browser opens a small window like this one. Select the line with your meter's name and press Pair.

That window belongs to the browser. It lists Bluetooth devices that are switched on near this computer, and gives this page access only to the one you select.

No meter was selected. If yours was not in the window: press a key on the meter to wake it, check that Bluetooth is off on your iPad or phone, wait a few seconds and try again.

Nothing is changed on your meter and nothing is uploaded. You get a small report file to email us; it holds the meter's name and model, the data it sent, and what you type here.
